# Wrong-direction case review: 29 October 2026

This is an investigation of six selected large movers, not an estimate of the system's overall accuracy. Values come from a read-only production query of the frozen `unified_recommendations_history` batch generated at 2026-10-28 17:00:01.919 UTC (22:30 IST), the 29 October OHLCV, and the existing retrospective. Close-to-close and open-to-close moves exclude costs. A negative call means the frozen batch's `Sell` classification, not proof that a short was executable. Historical explanations and precursor fields are mutable; they cannot establish what the system knew at the cutoff without independent timestamped evidence.

## Recommendation lifecycle: material findings

1. **Collection and freshness.** The actual 30 October integrity report included split-like OHLCV gaps, a failed backup and stale endpoint validation. Three Trendlyne symbols missing from the 29 October daily batch were recovered on 30 October with real provider fetches. A later successful fetch does not establish that its values were available to the prior decision; the split adjustments and backup recovery remain open (AF-20261029-95, AF-20261030-02, -08).
2. **Point-in-time features.** NSE result `broadcast_ts` establishes exchange publication, not system ingestion; `fetched_at` is overwritten. For BSOFT and AEROFLEX, however, the production XBRL rows currently show `parsed_at` 2026-10-28 16:07:53 UTC, corroborated by the 21:37:53 IST XBRL completion log, before the 17:00 UTC ranker batch. The current XBRL writer overwrites `parsed_at` on conflict. A local, undeployed change retains future parsed versions; its observation timestamp alone cannot establish commit visibility at the decision cutoff. Date-only screener snapshots can union multiple same-day runs, and precursor technical fields can be backfilled. Local prospective capture fixes do not reconstruct missing historical observations (AF-20261030-04, -10, -11, -15).
3. **Ranking and risk.** The canonical ranker blends component engines and applies overlays; the live ML contribution is excluded. The red-flag multiplier lowers a directional score as well as excluding buys, potentially creating a Sell from a risk warning. Its true counterfactual requires saved decision inputs and a frozen replay, not cherry-picked misses (AF-20261030-05).
4. **Publication.** Local fixes correct mixed-date MCP picks, misleading scanner examples and unvalidated ML probability labels. They await release gates and deployment (AF-20261029-05, -06; AF-20261030-06).
5. **Evaluation.** The old Signal Accuracy report used the wrong recommendation batch for some symbols and selected movers using an evaluation-day price floor. The locally corrected timing still produces a large-mover diagnostic, not an overall accuracy or net trading-profit estimate. Costs, actionable entry, missing history and overlapping horizons matter (AF-20261030-03, -09).

The latest reviewed frozen forward evidence remains LOW-DATA with negative net excess after costs. No change to score weights or directional classifications has been promoted based on these selected cases.

The linked-news table also contains result-related BSE announcements for both companies and a CNBC TV18 BSOFT results headline published on 28 October before the batch. This corrects the earlier, narrower check of stock-news links: it omitted exchange announcements and other news sources. Publication timestamps alone do not prove the link existed in our system by the batch; current `fetched_at` values can be overwritten. Aeroflex Enterprises announcements linking both AEROENTER and AEROFLEX are supported by their summaries, which explicitly refer to subsidiary Aeroflex Industries; this is not evidence of an entity-linking error.

The platform does have separate earnings beat/miss features sourced from `stock_earnings_beats` and written to `technical_signals`; those are not the same as direct, timestamped ingestion of these XBRL results into the ranker. Some related features are inputs to the ML ensemble, whose live ranking contribution is excluded. Other component engines might have reflected related information indirectly, but the frozen record does not establish the effect of either specific result on its score.
